# Enhanced theoretical best and per-improvement ranges

The coach automatically analyzes the fastest valid lap. Other valid laps supply supporting evidence; no target time is requested. All numerical measurements, ranges, selection membership and totals come from deterministic Rust analysis. The local model cannot calculate, change or fabricate a gain.

Each primary card uses `gainRange` with a disclosed `demonstrated` or `hypothesis` basis, fixed-distance method and assumptions. Unsupported ideas say **Gain not yet quantifiable**. A recorded gap or legacy point estimate alone is not displayed as a recoverable range. Scenario bounds express conditional engineering assumptions, not a confidence interval or a guaranteed minimum gain.

Demonstrated ranges require valid supporting section timing, uncertainty checks and boundary-state compatibility. Repeatability informs confidence. Full section timing accounts for entry, apex, exit and the following section where the detector window includes it. Conditional cross-lap scenarios retain both faster and slower portions of the supporting speed profile; a spatial taper restores baseline speeds at both boundaries. They no longer assume that every positive supporting speed advantage can be recovered for free.

Fastest-only recovery scenarios investigate prolonged minimum-to-+1 m/s recovery. Modest changes must fit sustained acceleration capacity recorded in a comparable speed band on the fastest lap. Fixed-distance time integration uses `integral (1/v_baseline - 1/v_scenario) dd` with metres and metres/second. Scenario start and end speeds match baseline; no downstream exit-speed benefit is extrapolated. Acceleration capacity does not measure throttle application, braking, steering, traction or an engine powerband.

`primaryOpportunityIds` selects at most three distinct, non-overlapping driving experiments in Rust. The full-lap budget considers all compatible fixed sections, independently of that display limit. Per section, a traditional-compatible observed estimate and endpoint-preserving modeled alternatives compete; a modeled alternative replaces the measured range only when it dominates both bounds. `enhancementBudget` records each chosen section range and its source. Only one range per section contributes to `improvementPotential`; measured and modeled estimates for the same section are alternatives and never added. `enhancedTheoreticalBest` is the fastest lap minus that full-lap range, with the upper gain producing the lower lap time. Traditional sector-stitching time remains a separate point reference in advanced details and is never added again. Uncertainty can straddle that observed point; the enhanced range is not forced below it by clipping away uncertainty. Missing or incompatible evidence leaves the total unavailable.

Gemma may order the selected set and interpret its evidence; it cannot substitute other opportunities or change the additive budget. Expanded card details disclose source observations, assumptions, uncertainty and validation. Inspection preserves the fastest lap's own absolute recording-time interval for plots, GPS and compatible video. Verify an experiment on repeated clean laps using full section, following-straight and whole-lap times; a local speed improvement can still produce a slower lap.

Fresh validation results for this revision are recorded in `enhanced-coach-validation.json`. Older time-estimate and redesign JSON artifacts describe historical protocols, not the current estimator.
